Directions

    Good to know: The pasta keeps absorbing broth, so leftovers get thick. Add a splash of water when reheating, or cook the pasta separately and add it per bowl.

    On the stove: an oval Dutch oven can go on the long center burner if the flame covers most of the base. If it's barely sizzling after a few minutes, move it to the largest round burner and stir now and then.
